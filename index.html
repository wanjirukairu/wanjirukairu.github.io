Kenyan writer, director, and producer working across film, television, animation, and audio. Writer and line producer of the International Emmy Award-winning production Jongo Love. Multi-award recipient across two decades of credits for M-Net, Showmax, Citizen TV, and LA-based production companies.

Founder of SetSync — the first software built to operate inside the film shoot day.

SetSync

The first software designed to operate inside the film shoot day. Every production tool stops at crew call. SetSync doesn't. Live coordination, real-time scene pivots, AI call sheet generation, and an auto wrap report that writes itself at picture wrap.
