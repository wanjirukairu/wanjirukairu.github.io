Writer · Director · Producer · Founder

Kenyan writer, director, and producer working across film, television, animation, and audio. Writer and line producer of Jongo Love, the International Emmy Award-winning audio drama. Multi-award recipient with credits for M-Net, Showmax, Citizen TV, and LA-based production companies spanning over two decades.
